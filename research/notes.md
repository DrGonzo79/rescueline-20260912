# Source notes

## Public concept

- **Source:** [Ideabrowser public idea URL](https://www.ideabrowser.com/hub/ideas/callback-list-for-callers-your-ai-receptionist-lost-609e344e)
- **Source date:** 2026-09-12
- **Idea:** build a morning callback list for small businesses using AI receptionists. Classify post-call records as handled, follow-up, or lost; show where the interaction failed; recommend a callback, text, or quote; and track recovered bookings.
- The source says AI phone answering adoption among small businesses increased 89% year over year and cites a claim that 85% of callers who do not get what they need never call back. These are source claims, not independently validated here.
- The source names Vapi and Retell as possible structured post-call data providers and says call-analysis products can cost hundreds of dollars per month.

## Prototype interpretation

RescueLine narrows the concept to plumbing and HVAC operators already using an AI receptionist. It demonstrates deterministic call triage over fictional records. No real model, telephony vendor, customer data, outbound message, CRM, booking, or payment integration exists.
